EXPLORED

I'm curious about ideas, people and the world around me. I enjoy exploring things beyond my regular studies, understanding how they work and finding ways to make learning more meaningful.

1. SnapShare – Solving a Problem Within My Community

At Navgurukul, I noticed a common problem during campus celebrations. Many people take photographs, but sharing them individually can be difficult. Sharing all the photos in a group also creates unnecessary storage problems for others.

This inspired me to build SnapShare, a campus photo-sharing platform where people can create events, upload photographs and view or download the pictures they want.

The project is almost complete, and I plan to test it during our next campus celebration.

2. Exploring Better Ways of Learning

I am interested in understanding how people learn and how we can make difficult concepts easier to understand.

I often explore questions like:

  • How can we explain a difficult concept in a simple way?
  • How can real-life examples make learning easier?
  • Why do different people understand the same concept differently?
  • How can we make learning more engaging and meaningful?

I enjoy experimenting with different ways of explaining things and helping others understand concepts.

3. Exploring Learning Through Activities

I have also explored how discussions, stories and activities can encourage people to think, express themselves and connect ideas with their own experiences.

This interest led me to design and conduct several English activities, where participants could share their thoughts, discuss different perspectives and learn from one another.

For me, exploration is not limited to textbooks or a particular subject. It is about observing things, asking questions, trying ideas and learning through experiences.
