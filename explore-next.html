WHAT I WANT TO EXPLORE

Understanding How People Learn

I want to explore pedagogy and understand how people learn.

I often find myself wondering:

  • Why do some people understand a concept quickly while others struggle with the same thing?
  • What makes a concept difficult to understand?
  • How can we explain something so that people can understand it more easily?
  • Can an activity help someone understand a concept better than simply explaining it?
  • What kind of activities work best for different concepts and learners?

I want to understand how people learn differently and what makes learning easier, more interesting and meaningful.

Exploring Activity-Based Learning

I want to learn how to turn ideas and concepts into practical, engaging activities.

For example:

  • How can we teach scientific concepts through experiments and hands-on activities?
  • How can we design activities that make people curious and encourage them to ask questions?
  • How can we make learning interactive instead of making people just listen?
  • How do we know whether an activity has actually helped someone understand a concept?

I want to explore different activity-design methods, experiment with ideas, observe how people respond and improve activities based on what I learn.
